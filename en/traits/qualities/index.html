    TRAITS¶

    Think of Traits as the answers to important questions about your character that are not covered by Powers or Knowledge:

    • What do you have? (Resources, a secret base, a special vehicle)

    • Who do you know? (Contacts, allies, affiliation with an organization)

    • Who are you? (A reputation, striking beauty, a well-kept secret identity)

    • What can you do exceptionally well? (An innate talent beyond normal training)

    In this quickstart, we provide Simple Traits, which cost 4 PAX each.

    Striking Appearance¶

    Your appearance is extraordinary, almost hypnotic. Once per Scene, when making a social test based on persuasion or seduction, you may spend 2 Resolve and automatically succeed.

    Polyglot¶

    You read, write, and speak all common human languages. In addition, your ease is so great that you may learn new languages simply by observing them for at least one Scene.

    Reliable Contact¶

    You have a guaranteed source of information (reporter, scientist, hacker, etc.). Once per Issue, you can ask the Editor to provide you with a useful clue from your Reliable Contact with relation to an investigation or mystery.

    Tough as Nails¶

    You are hard to bring down. Gain Damage Reduction 2, and when you mark the Defeated Condition, you may still act for one more Turn before falling.

    Catchphrase¶

    Define a Catchphrase together with the Editor. Once per Scene, when you shout it loud and clear, choose one of the benefits below:

    • Reduce the Resolve cost of an ability by a value equal to your Tier.

    • Gain Advantage on your next roll.

    Pocket Gadgets¶

    You always have the right item ready for every situation. Once per Scene, you may spend 1 Resolve to receive one Gear item from the list.

    Voice of the Crowd¶

    In times of crisis, your voice stands out. When you choose this Trait, you receive Advantage on tests involving giving instructions, controlling, and calming crowds in Rescue Scenes.

    Influencer¶

    You have many followers on social media. Maybe you have a channel where you post your patrols, a page where you review the latest heroic products or simply a fan page for your pet.

    ‘’Today’s sponsor is…’’ : Once per Issue, you may receive a Freebie, which is a small item such as a top-of-the-Line cellphone, a small weapon or an item of clothing.

    Secret Identity¶

    You keep your civilian persona well protected and separate from your heroic life, whether through a mask or hypnotic glasses. No one (or almost no one) knows your identity. You gain Advantage on tests involving your civilian life, such as talking about the weather, standing in line, or taking the bus.

    Put on the Glasses: Once per Issue, when you are engaged in some heroic activity, you may activate your civilian persona to leave the Scene quickly. When you do, you may reappear in an Adjacent Zone.

    Dynamic Duo¶

    You have forged a powerful and genuine bond with another Hero. This Trait only functions if both Heroes involved possess it, and the Dynamic Duo only functions between the two of you. Whenever you are in the same Scene, both of you receive +1 Resolve, Accuracy, and Defense. In addition, whenever the Hero to whom you are linked suffers any amount of Damage, you receive 1 point of Damage.

    Synchronized Attack: Once per Issue, the Dynamic Duo may make a powerful joint strike. For this Attack, both make separate Attack rolls, keeping the best result. Take each Hero’s Attribute that they used for the Attack, add them together, and divide the total by 2. The result (rounded down) is the Damage Bonus for the Attack.

    Affiliation¶

    You are a member of an organization such as the Bat-Family or Steelworks. When using the fame of your affiliation in social Tests, you may spend 2 Resolve to gain Advantage on that Test.

    Aid from the Group: Once per Issue, you may call for aid, such as receiving a technological device from Steelworks or access to Gotham’s most exclusive clubs through the Bat-Family.
