    Introduction & Core Concepts¶

    INTRODUCTION¶

    Hello!

    My name is Mark Waid. I have written nearly a thousand DC comics (and counting), researched and produced the 2025 miniseries New History of the DC Universe, which recounted in meticulous detail DC continuity from its earliest beginnings to the present day, and, as a fan and researcher, I have read more DC comics than you probably imagine exist. As someone who has been passionate about the adventures of Superman, Batman, Wonder Woman and all their friends and enemies since the age of three, I know this universe like the back of my hand. That is why I consider it a tremendous privilege to present to you: Justice League Unlimited RPG, the most ambitious, most comprehensive and most up-to-date DC roleplaying system ever produced.

    WHAT IS THIS GAME?¶

    This is a quickstart, a fast version of Justice League Unlimited Roleplaying, a roleplaying game set in the fantastic universe of DC Comics, where powerful Kryptonians fight side by side with determined Amazons and unstoppable humans.

    Imagine a game of make-believe, but with rules that help to support the narrative.

    A GAME OF HEROES¶

    Heroes are at the center of Justice League Unlimited. Heroes are the light in the darkness, beacons of hope amid the indifference of the world, and they do what is right.

    Doing what is right goes far beyond foiling the plans of an evil villain: it means protecting entire cities, saving victims of natural disasters, and much more. Heroes inspire, fight to defend, and help everyone from the smallest insects to the mightiest worlds.

    HOW DO I PLAY THIS?¶

    In a roleplaying game like this, the first and most important step is to gather your own Justice League: your friends. With your team assembled, you will need the tools of the trade: a set of polyhedral dice (d20, d12, d8 and d6), paper for your hero sheets, a pencil, an eraser and, most crucial of all, a boundless imagination.

    One of you will take up the special mantle of Editor. This person is responsible for presenting the story, giving voice to every citizen in danger and every megalomaniacal villain, and proposing the challenges that will test the heroes’ courage. And yes, from time to time, it is the Editor’s duty to do the funny voice of the little old lady who was rescued from a burning building and cannot stop thanking her saviors.

    THE DICE¶

    In the JLU RPG, fate is shaped by courage, but chance has its say. Its voice belongs to the dice. The game uses four different types of dice to determine the outcome of uncertain actions:

    • the twenty-sided die (d20),

    • the twelve-sided die (d12),

    • the eight-sided die (d8),

    • the six-sided die (d6),

    • and sometimes 1d3, which is a d6 divided in half (1-2 = 1, 3-4 = 2, 5-6 = 3).

    ROLLING DICE¶

    The basic mechanic that governs moments of uncertainty in the JLU RPG is the roll of a twenty-sided die (d20). Whenever a Hero attempts an action whose outcome is uncertain or whose failure carries real risk, they must make a Test.

    This result is then compared to a target number defined by the Editor, known as Tension.

    THE EDITOR¶

    Every great comic book saga is guided by the hand of a good editor, and in Justice League UnLimited RPG this tradition continues. The Editor is responsible for presenting the story, giving voice to every citizen in peril and every megalo-maniacal villain, and proposing the challenges that will test the heroes’ courage. As a good Editor, you are not the players’ adversary - you are your Heroes’ biggest fan, but you roleplay their enemies.

    THE HEROES¶

    Alongside a great Editor, captivating protagonists are the driving force behind any great comic book. In JLU RPG, you are those protagonists. By playing a Hero, you take on the leading role in a saga, interacting with the dynamic world proposed by the Editor, overcoming impossible challenges, and forging connections with those you save and with your teammates.

    CORE CONCEPTS¶

    ISSUE¶

    An Issue is the name given to a JLU game session. It begins when everyone gathers to play, and the Editor starts narrating the streets of Metropolis or the alleyways of Gotham. It ends at that perfect moment of tension with a ‘to be continued in the next issue…’ feeling.

    SCENE¶

    Throughout the game, you will come across the term “scene” many times. A scene is a narrative segment, such as a confrontation in the streets of Gotham or a moment of relaxation in the Watchtower cafeteria. If you are unsure about how long a scene lasts, think of the pages of a comic book: if, from one page to the next, the story’s narrator adds “meanwhile” or “Metropolis-8:00 AM” in the corner of the panel, a new scene has begun.

    THE TIERS¶

    Tiers are the foundation of much of JLU RPG: Heroes, villains and dangers are categorized according to them. Think of a Tier as a kind of “level” that beings in this universe occupy. But remember: supernatural abilities and fantastic powers are not the only things taken into account: Their impact on the world, experience, and even popularity all factor into this dynamic.

    Tier E represents the lowest level, comparable to the power of an ordinary human. Heroes and Villains begin to be classified starting at Tier D. Other threats, such as natural disasters, experiments gone out of control, alien invasions and humanitarian crises, also follow the same classification logic.

    E - Ordinary human.

    Basic threat or danger, such as theft or assault with a bladed weapon.

    D - Beginning of meta-activity. Novice vigilantes, incidents involving lower-scale powers.

    C - Urban level.

    Individuals capable of wreaking havoc on an entire city.

    B - Powerful enough to trouble a nation.

    Heroes at this level face small armies and possess advanced abilities.

    A - Global level.

    Capable of interacting with and impacting the entire planet.

    S - Legendary level.

    The mere mention of the name is enough to shake the multiverse.

    ATTRIBUTES¶

    In JLU RPG, Attributes are the basic measures of a Hero, representing their fundamental capabilities in different areas such as strength, dexterity and ingenuity. Attributes play a central role in the system, as they serve as the foundation for dice rolls and the resolution of actions during play. The six Attributes are Potency, Accuracy, Agility, Resistance, Mind and Spirit.

    RESOLVE¶

    Resolve is a vital resource for a Hero, as it represents both their ability to keep fighting and the energy required for extraordinary feats, activating powers, or resisting adverse effects. In practical terms, Resolve measures how much energy your Hero has to keep enduring blows, as well as how much energy they have to fuel their powers and abilities. For example, a Hero may start a Round with 15 Resolve points but then they might take a blow that deals 6 points of Resolve damage (15-6=9). In the same Round, the Hero activates their Blast power, spending 2 more Resolve points (9-2=7), leaving them with 7 points remaining.

    Resolve is calculated using your Tier base value plus your Spirit value.

    CONDITIONS¶

    Conditions are dangerous states a Hero can enter. Each Hero has 5 Condition Boxes. When Resolve runs out, the character marks a Condition. When an attack deals 10 or more points of damage at once, a Hero or Villain may choose to mark a Condition instead of losing Resolve.

    Tired: The fight is dragging on; you start to gasp and lose your breath.

    Scratched: It was just a scratch. There’s still plenty of fuel left to burn. Apply -2 to all d20 rolls.

    Injured: That hurt. Not enough to stop you, but it hurts. Apply -4 to all d20 rolls.

    Wounded: Time to start worrying. Apply Disadvantage to all d20 rolls.

    Defeated: The Hero is out of action and at the Editor’s mercy. Their fate will be decided by the course of the story.

    POWERS¶

    Powers are what define your Hero’s fantastic abilities, whether that means fighting with swords, firing blasts from their hands, controlling fire, or casting spells. In this quickstart, we present three categories of Powers: Superpowers, which include mutations and abilities derived from biology; Martial Training, which covers everything from the use of staffs, and one’s bare hands, to ranged weapons like guns; and Technology, which, when sufficiently advanced, is indistinguishable from any superhuman ability. Each Power has Grades ranging from 1 to 5, limited by a character’s Tier.
