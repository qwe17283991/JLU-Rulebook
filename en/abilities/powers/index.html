    SUPERPOWERS¶

    Whether firing beams from your eyes, controlling the elements, changing your appearance at will, or teleporting: all of these fit within the Superpowers category. As the broadest category, these are usually what we think of when imagining the concept of superheroes. This subgroup refers to the natural characteristics of a Hero. They are the result of genetic mutations or inheritance.

    ANIMAL MIMICRY¶

    3 PAX / Grade¶

    You can channel the Red, the force that connects all animal life, to manifest the traits of beasts such as claws, wings, hide, and hooves. When using this power, you may manifest a number of Animal Adaptations equal to your Grade, spending 1 Resolve per activated Adaptation. These adaptations last for the Scene.

    Grade 1¶

    Beast Claws¶

    (Adaptation)

    You manifest natural weapons. Your Damage Die increases by one step when using unarmed attacks.

    Reinforced Hide¶

    (Adaptation)

    Your skin becomes as thick as a rhinoceros’ hide. You gain a Defense bonus equal to your Grade in this power, +1.

    Bear Strength¶

    (Adaptation)

    You channel the strength of a great animal. You gain a bonus to your Potency Tests equal to your Grade+2.

    Feline Agility¶

    (Adaptation)

    You move with the grace of a predator. You gain Advantage on your Agility Tests.

    Grade 2¶

    Eagle Wings¶

    (Adaptation)

    You manifest wings, gaining Flight at a Grade equal to your Grade in this power (up to a maximum of 4).

    Hunter Senses¶

    (Adaptation)

    Your senses sharpen. You may manifest a wolf’s sense of smell or the keen sight of an eagle, gaining Advantage on tests involving smell or sight.

    ELASTICITY¶

    5 PAX / Grade¶

    Your body has no fixed form and is completely malleable. A powerful blow would smash your head ? You flatten yourself on the spot. Is the remote control too far away to reach? You stretch your arms to grab it. The sky (or your imagination, if we are being honest) is the Limit.

    Grade 1¶

    Malleable Body¶

    (Passive)

    You can flatten yourself to pass under doors and through cracks, and absorb damage. You gain Damage Reduction equal to your Grade in this power +1 against Physical damage.

    Extended Reach¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 1 Resolve

    You are able to stretch your limbs. When doing so, you may use them to extend your reach up to Close range. You spend 1 additional Resolve point for each Round you keep Extended Reach active.

    Parachute Form¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: Resolve

    You can transform into a wide, flattened shape, like a parachute or glider. You can carry a number of allies equal to your Grade in this power, +2 and safely glide from any height.

    Grade 2¶

    Bounce Back¶

    Activation: Reaction

    Cost: 2 Resolve

    When hit by an Attack, you may assume a ball-Like form, absorbing part of the impact. When doing so, you are knocked back 1 distance and take half the Damage dealt.

    Elastic Prison¶

    Activation: 2 Actions

    Cost: 2 Determination

    You wrap an adjacent target. The target gains the “Immobilized” status and must succeed on an Opposed Potency Test against your Resistance to escape. You gain a bonus equal to your Grade in this power on the Test.

    ELEMENTAL MANIPULATION¶

    5 PAX / Grade¶

    You are able to create, manipulate, amplify and diminish an element. Choose one element among Fire, Ice, Water, Air, Earth, Electricity, and Toxic. Each type of element must be purchased separately, and whenever you use your element for Tests, such as creating an ice ramp to save civilians or manipulating fire to reduce a blaze, you gain +2 per Grade in this power on your Tests.

    When choosing this Power, decide when adding it to your sheet whether the Damage it causes is based on Potency or Spirit.

    Grade 1¶

    Elemental Ray¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 1 Resolve

    You make a Ranged Attack with Close range using your element. This Attack gains a bonus to hit and Damage equal to your Grade in this Power. When you achieve a Splash Page, the target gains the Status appropriate to your element:

    Fire: Burned

    Ice: Frozen

    Earth: Immobilized Electricity: Stunned Toxic: Poisoned

    Elemental Shield¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 1 Resolve

    You use your element to create a protective barrier, creating a shield that protects you and a number of allies equal to your Grade in this power, providing a bonus to Defense equal to your Grade in this Power +1. The Elemental Shield is immobile. Solid elements such as Earth and Ice provide 2 + your Grade in Defense. When an Attack hits you, the shield is dismissed.

    Grade 2¶

    Elemental Propulsion¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 2 Resolve

    You use your element to propel yourself. Until the end of your next Turn, you may move 2 Distances instead of 1 with movement actions.

    Elementalize¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 2 Resolve

    You spend 2 Resolve to force the target to make an Opposed Resistance Test against your Spirit. If the target fails, it suffers the Status related to your element until the beginning of your next Turn.

    Fire: Engulfed Water: Slowed Earth: Prone Air: Slowed Ice: Immobilized Electricity: Stunned Toxic: Engulfed

    ENERGY BLAST¶

    4 PAX / Grade¶

    This power encompasses different forms of energy projection, such as firing lasers from the eyes, energy blasts from the hands, or even charged breath from the mouth. When you gain this power, decide whether the Damage is based on Potency or Spirit.

    A Hero with this power may choose to make a simple ranged attack using their Damage Die without spending Resolve and without receiving any extra benefits from Energy Blast.

    Grade 1¶

    Energy Shot¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 1 Resolve

    You fire energy as a Ranged Attack. You attempt to hit an Adjacent or Close enemy with an energy blast. The Attack and Damage gain a bonus of 1 + your Grade in Energy Blast.

    Grade 2¶

    Brutal Blast¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 2 Resolve

    When you hit a target with your Energy Blast, the target must make an Opposed Resistance Test against your Potency or Spirit. If they fail, the target is pushed 1 Distance.

    ENHANCED RESISTANCE¶

    3 PAX per Grade¶

    You possess a body more durable than normal, whether through hardened skin, metal plates, natural scales, cybernetic armor or superhuman constitution.

    Grade 1¶

    Robust Body¶

    Passive

    Your Resistance increases by +2, and your Damage Reduction increases by +1 per Grade you possess in Enhanced Resistance.

    Brace for Impact¶

    Activation: 2 Actions

    Cost: 1 Resolve

    You prepare yourself for an imminent impact. Until the beginning of your next turn, you may add your Enhanced Resistance Grade to your Damage Reduction against the next attack you receive.

    Grade 2¶

    Superhuman Vigor¶

    Passive

    Your body becomes a self-sufficient and durable system. You become immune to all poisons and diseases of non-magical origin. In addition, you can hold your breath for several hours and gain resistance to environments of extreme heat and cold.

    FLIGHT¶

    3 PAX / Grade¶

    You are capable of taking to the skies, reaching incredible speeds.

    Grade 1¶

    Basic Flight¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 1 Resolve

    You take flight and may move through the air. While in the air, you gain a bonus equal to your Grade in this power on your Agility Tests. For each Round after the first in which you remain airborne, you must spend 1 Resolve.

    Superhero landing¶

    Passive

    When you enter a combat Scene by landing from a flight, you gain a bonus to your Initiative roll equal to your Grade in Flight.

    Grade 2¶

    Aerial Ride¶

    Passive

    You are strong enough to carry one ally with you while flying, with no penalty to speed.

    Cruising Speed¶

    Passive

    If you possess the Superhuman Speed power, your flight speed becomes as fast as your running speed. You may use the “Dash” movement ability from Superhuman Speed.

    FORCE FIELD¶

    3 PAX / Grade¶

    You become capable of generating powerful force fields. They can be used to block attacks, as platforms, and even to imprison enemies.

    Grade 1¶

    Field Generation¶

    Activation: 1 Action (or Reaction)

    Cost: 1 Resolve

    You generate a Force Field targeting yourself. It provides +2 Defense and Damage Reduction 1. The Force Field lasts for 1 Round. This ability may be used as a Reaction, as long as you are able to use it in that manner. Alternatively, you may generate the field on a target Adjacent to you.

    I Won’t Hold Much longer!¶

    Activation: Reaction

    Cost: 1 Resolve

    When you dramatically declare that your force field will not hold much longer, it gains a bonus equal to twice your Grade in this Power to Defense and Damage Reduction. The Force Field is dismissed at the end of this Turn.

    Saving Field¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 1 Resolve

    When you use your Force Fields in Heroic Actions during Rescue Scenes, you may protect a small group of civilians, gaining a +2 bonus per Grade on Actions involving your field.

    Grade 2¶

    Expanded Protection¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 2 Resolve

    Your Field Generation increases to +3 Defense and Damage Reduction 2, and may also affect 1 target within Close distance of you.

    Platform¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 2 Resolve

    Your force fields may be used as static platforms for movement and tactical positioning. You create a platform at any point you can see within a Close distance. It can support a number of targets equal to your Spirit value (minimum 1) and lasts for 1 Round.

    SPEED FORCE¶

    12 PAX per Grade¶

    The Speed Force is one of the seven fundamental forces of the universe. It is the manifestation of movement and speed. Those touched by it are not mere speedsters, but true avatars of velocity: it allows incredible feats beyond simply running at absurd speeds, such as temporal manipulation, molecular vibration and even extremely accelerated thought.

    Grade 1¶

    Kinetic Connection¶

    Activation: Passive

    Your connection to the Speed Force grants you a +3 bonus to Agility per Grade you possess in this power.

    Kinetic Impact¶

    Activation: Passive

    Speed is your weapon. When you spend a Movement Action before making a Melee Attack, you may use your Agility modifier instead of Potency to deal Damage.

    Accelerated Mind¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 1 Resolve

    You can process information at super speed. If you have access to a data source (a book, a computer, a crime scene), you can absorb all details in a fraction of a second, gaining Advantage on your next Mind Test to analyze or learn about that subject.

    Accelerated Movement¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 1 Resolve

    You may move two Distances with a single Movement Action.

    Grade 2¶

    Barrage of Blows¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 2 Resolve

    You unleash a series of punches faster than the eye can see. With a single Attack roll, you strike the opponent multiple times, dealing +1 Damage Die against them.

    Instant Punch¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 2 Resolve You make a Melee

    Attack against any target up to a Far distance away from you.

    Scarlet Tornado¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 2 Resolve

    You spin your arms or run in circles at high speed, creating a vortex of air.

    Make an Attack roll against all targets within a Close distance. You deal Damage equal to half your Agility value (rounded up) to the targets. Alternatively, you may choose to pull or push the targets 1 Distance.

    Accelerated Burst¶

    Activation: Free

    Cost: 2 Resolve

    You force yourself to go even faster, gaining +1 Movement Action that Turn or +1 Dodge. At the end of the Scene, mark one Condition.

    Using the Speed Force

    Although there are other powers related to speed, the Speed Force still reigns supreme. In narrative terms, a Hero (or Villain) with this power is capable of unique feats. Sweeping through an entire city in seconds, reaching the other side of the planet and returning in the same instant, the list is virtually endless.

    For reasons of rules, balance, and game design in general, we could not allow a literal transcription of the Force: it would be impossible. As Narrator, adopt the principle that, following the Grade chart at the beginning of this chapter, if a user of this power thinks of a feat of speed, they can do it, as long as they succeed on a simple Test.

    A character may not possess both Speed Force and Superhuman Speed under any circumstances.

    SUPER STRENGTH¶

    3 PAX per Grade¶

    This is a classic power that allows herculean feats of strength such as lifting heavy vehicles, removing massive debris, breaking walls with a single punch, and much more. Super

    Strength is more than a simple increase to Potency; it allows its wielder to perform some extraordinary physical feats.

    Grade 1¶

    Brute Strength¶

    Passive

    Your Potency increases by +2 per Grade in Super Strength.

    Powerful Strike¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 1 Resolve

    When you make a Melee Attack, you may activate Powerful Strike. If it hits, you roll one additional Damage Die.

    Grade 2¶

    Saving Strength¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 2 Resolve

    When in Rescue Scenes and using your Super Strength in Heroic Actions, you gain Advantage on your Tests.

    SUPERHUMAN SPEED¶

    3 PAX / Grade¶

    You are fast. Very fast. Although not as fast as someone touched by the Speed Force, your speed still allows you to perform incredible feats of agility and momentum. A character may not possess both Superhuman Speed and Speed Force.

    Grade 1¶

    Natural Runner¶

    Passive

    Your Agility increases by +2 per Grade you possess in Superhuman Speed.

    Dash¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 1 Resolve

    While in contact with the ground, you may concentrate your speed into a single burst, moving faster than the eye can follow. You may move up to two Distances instead of one.

    Grade 2¶

    Quick Reflexes¶

    Passive

    Your reflexes are enhanced, allowing you to react to danger before most people even realize it exists. You add your Grade in Superhuman Speed to your Initiative rolls.

    Teleportation¶

    5 PAX/Grade¶

    You can bend space or pierce dimensions to travel from point A to point B instantaneously, ignoring any physical barriers between them.

    Grade 1¶

    Spatial Sense¶

    Activation: Passive

    Your brain processes space at hyper-accelerated speed so you don’t appear inside a wall.

    You receive a bonus equal to your Grade in this Power on Agility checks that involve moving and jumping. Additionally, you receive a Bonus on your Defense equal to your Grade in this Power, +1.

    Tactical Jump¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 1 Resolve

    You “blink” in space, teleporting yourself to any point you can see within Near range. On Rescue Scenes, you ignore any difficult terrain, chasms, or high walls, you don’t need to roll to reach victims in precarious locations (like the top of a burning building or a cliff), you simply appear there.

    Space Warp¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 1 Resolve

    You use teleport to instantly flank and teleport to a space Adjacent to an enemy, performing a Melee Attack in the same movement with Advantage.

    Grade 2¶

    Quick Extraction¶

    Activation: 1 Action

    Cost: 2 Resolve

    You touch an ally, civilian, or object (up to the size of a motorcycle) and leap through space with it to any Distant point. If you attempt to teleport an enemy, make an Opposed Agility Test against the Potency or Agility of the target whichever is higher.

    Evasive Teleport¶

    Activation: Reactio

    Cost: 2 Resolve

    When you are targeted by an attack (melee or ranged), you can disappear one millisecond before impact and add your Teleportation Grade to your Block or Dodge against that Attack. If the attack misses, you reappear at any point within Near range.
