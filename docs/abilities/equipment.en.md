# EQUIPMENT

## Weapons

In JLU RPG, there is no long and detailed list of every existing weapon; here we use a simpler and more straightforward approach, dividing them into two categories: **Martial Weapons** and **Ranged Weapons**. Each of these categories includes a different set of armaments. Whenever a Hero acquires a weapon, it should be noted on their character sheet, and the player might even want to give the weapon a unique name. Otherwise, something like “my trident” or “my boomerang” would suffice.”

## Martial Weapons

##### 1 PAX each

These are weapons effective in melee combat, such as swords, staffs, and tridents, used as a true extension of their wielder. Those who know how to use martial weapons with mastery treat them as another part of their body. While using a martial weapon, you gain **+1 Damage**. This bonus is not cumulative with other weapons.

## Ranged Weapons

##### 1 PAX each

Ranged weapons include any weapon capable of firing projectiles, such as bows and arrows, boomerangs, or energy weapons. Each Ranged Weapon grants a **+1 bonus to Attack**. This bonus is not cumulative with other weapons.

**Range**: ALL Ranged Weapons have **Range**, which indicates how far an Attack with them can reach. ALL ranged weapons have a **Close** range, and any Attack made at another distance suffers **Disadvantage**. A weapon’s range may be increased to **Far** for **5 PAX**.

## Special Material

The universe is permeated by many different materials, each with unique and impactful properties: a mace made of **Nth Metal** can grant flight, while a sword made of **Kryptonite** can be an extremely dangerous weapon against Kryptonians. In martial weapons, these materials appear as part of the weapon’s composition; in ranged weapons, they appear as ammunition.

**Ammunition:** Ammunition is essential for users of ranged weapons: imagine Green Arrow walking around without a good bundle of arrows! Even so, in Justice League Unlimited RPG, we do not require a Hero to keep track of how many shots they still have left, but in the case of **Special Material**, things work a little differently. For that, we adopt the **Ammunition Die**. It is equal to your **Tier Damage Die**, and at the end of a Scene in which you used the special material, you roll that die. If the result is **1 or less**, your ammunition has run out and you need to get more!

##### Kryptonite

**Cost:** 5 PAX

Your weapon contains fragments of the mineral that is anathema to Kryptonians.

##### Nth Metal

**Cost:** 3 PAX

This metal native to the planet Thanagar has properties capable of negating gravity. A weapon made from this material grants **Flight Grade 1** to its wielder.

##### Promethium

**Cost:** 7 PAX

This metallic alloy is the strongest material on planet Earth. **Promethium** weapons ignore an amount of a target’s **Damage Reduction** equal to the wielder’s **Tier**.

## GEAR

##### 1 PAX each

These are useful gadgets with a variety of uses. They range from communicators and smoke bombs all the way to grappling guns. Gear has 3 uses, which recharge at the beginning of each Issue, and using them requires 1 Action.

**Smoke Bombs:** Spend 1 Use; for **1 Round**, all targets within a **Close** Zone of where you threw the bomb suffer **–3** on rolls that require sight.

**Grappling Gun:** When you spend one of its uses, you may move **1 Distance** in any direction.

**Pull and Strike:** You may spend 1 use to make a **Ranged Attack** against a target at **Close** or **Far** distance from you. If it hits, you move the target **1 Distance** toward you. If the target ends up Adjacent to you, you deal **Damage**.

**Healing Spray:** Spend 1 use to remove the **Scratched** or **Tired** Conditions from an **Adjacent** target.

**Tracker:** Spend 1 use to place the Tracker on a person or object. It emits a GPS signal that precisely indicates the target’s location.

**Rebreather:** Spend 1 use; until the end of the Scene, you are able to breathe normally in aquatic environments.

**Alteration Mask:** Spend 1 use; until the end of the Scene, you are able to alter your face.

**E.M.P. (Electromagnetic Pulse):** Spend 1 use; all non-military electronic devices within a **Close** distance are disabled for one Scene. Robotic enemies or those wearing technological armor (such as an **Exoskeleton**) must make a **Resistance Test** against **Tension 16** or lose access to all of their technological abilities for **1 Round**.

**Adhesive Bomb:** Spend 1 use and choose a target up to **Close** distance away. The target must succeed on an **Agility Test** against **Tension 15** or gain the “**Immobilized**” Status.

**Personal Hologram:** Spend 1 use to activate the hologram, which appears in a location at **Close** distance. Until the beginning of your next turn, any enemy who tries to attack you must first pass a **Mind Test** against **Tension** **14**. On a failure, they attack the hologram and lose the Action.

**Cryptographic Sequencer:** Spend 1 use before making a test to hack a system, disable an electronic lock, or decipher a coded message. You gain **Advantage** on that test.

**Sleep Gas:** Spend 1 use. ALL targets within **Close** distance (excluding you) must succeed on a **Resistance Test** against **Tension 14** or gain the “**Asleep**” Status for **1 Round** or until they suffer damage.
