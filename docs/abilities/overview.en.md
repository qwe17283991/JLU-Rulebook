# POWERS

## Acquiring Powers

A Hero’s **Origin** defines which Powers are within their reach. In addition, each Power has each power has a **PAX** cost, indicated in its description. The cost refers to one **Grade** of that Power; for example, if a Power has a cost of **3 PAX**, you must spend **6 PAX** to have it at **Grade 2**. When you acquire a Grade in a given Power, you gain all the abilities listed at that Grade, unless otherwise specified.

###### Using Powers

Each Power provides a set of abilities according to its Grade (see below). When a Hero acquires a Grade in that Power, they gain access to all of them. The use of these abilities requires an **Activation**, which is the number of Actions spent on that Power, and a **Resolve Cost**, which must be paid when the ability is used. Some abilities have the **Passive** descriptor, which indicates that they are always active.

**Cost**: This is the amount of **Resolve** spent to use a given Power. As a rule, the cost can never be reduced below **1 Resolve**. Some specific abilities, such as the **Champion of Tomorrow’s Classic Heroism**, clearly state that there is no Resolve cost and can therefore remove the cost entirely.

**Duration**: Some powers create effects whose duration is given in their description; this indicates how long the power remains active. Duration may vary between **Turns**, **Rounds**, or even the entire **Scene**.

## GRADES

Each Grade provides a set of abilities, and a Hero of that Grade has access to all of them. These abilities have an **Activation**, which indicates the number of actions spent to activate them, and a **Resolve cost** in their description. Some abilities are **Passive**, which means they do not require activation, having continuous or specific effects that happen without requiring the user to spend actions.

##### Grade 1 — The Whole Neighborhood!

Represents the most basic level of that power. A **Pyrokinetic** can control small flames and manipulate bonfires, a **Martial Master** has just completed their training, and a **Speedster** can cross several city blocks at an impressive speed.

##### Grade 2 — The Whole City!

Things are starting to get more interesting.

A **Grade 2 Illusionist** is capable of creating illusions the size of houses, a **Swordsman** has enough skill to rival the greatest masters in the country, and someone with **Super Strength** can throw cars as if they were made of paper.

## CATEGORIES

### Martial Training

This category encompasses specialisation in many different forms of combat. Whether that be the monastic learning of traditional martial arts, military training focused on guerilla tactics and armed combat, or the mastery of a melee weapon.

### Superpowers

This is the broadest category: everything from flying to firing beams from your eyes, metamorphosis, and even manipulating emotions. **Superpowers** are innate abilities or the result of a mutation; they are intrinsic to a being.

### Technology

These are gadgets, technological armor, special weapons, and much more. Technology sufficiently advanced... well, you know the rest: anything tech and electronic, human or alien.
