# TECHNOLOGY

Technology sufficiently advanced is as impactful as any superpower. In the universe of **JLU RPG**, it can equal or surpass superhuman abilities. Imagine a technological armor capable of rivalling Kryptonian strength, running on the most sophisticated quantum computers, and bearing weapons with as much firepower as any optical blast.

Technologies are powerful pieces of equipment that provide many different capabilities to their users: in a connected world, someone with access to the right devices can influence global events, neutralize gigantic threats and simulate powers possessed only by the most extraordinary beings. From the **Dark Knight** who uses **Technological Spheres** to investigate a crime scene to the **Genius** who uses an **Exoskeleton** to fly at rocket speed, technology is a powerful front.

## EXOSKELETON

##### 8 PAX / Grade

This armor (yes, mechanically the **Exoskeleton** counts as armor*) enhances the abilities of whoever wears it, transforming an ordinary person into a one-man army. When you acquire this power, you build your **Exoskeleton** by choosing **two Improvements** from the list below. With each new Grade, you may add **one more Improvement**.

The use of these abilities is covered by the normal Action cost, unless otherwise specified. The activation cost is **1 Resolve for Grade 1; 2 for Grade 2; 4 for Grade 3; 6 for Grade 4; and 8** for Grade 5.

If your **Resolve** reaches **0**, your armor enters emergency mode, disabling all of its **Improvements** until it is recharged.

##### Strength Systems

(Passive)

Powerful servomotors increase your strength. At **Grade 1**, your **Potency** becomes **10**. For each subsequent Grade, gain **+3 Potency**.

##### Integrated Weaponry

(Passive)

Your suit has an integrated weapons system. You gain the benefits of the **Energy Blast** power at a Grade equal to your **Exoskeleton Grade**.

Using **Energy Blast** costs **1 Action** and the **Resolve cost** corresponding to the Grade used.

##### Flight Thrusters

(Passive)

Your armor has a flight system. You gain the **Flight** power at a Grade equal to your **Exoskeleton Grade**.

##### Force Field Generator

(Passive)

The suit can project an energy barrier. You gain the **Force Field** power at a Grade equal to your **Exoskeleton Grade**. Using **Force Field** costs **1 Action** and the **Resolve cost** corresponding to the Grade used.

##### Composite Armor

(Passive)

The armor is made from an ultra-resistant alloy. You gain a permanent **+1 Defense per Grade in Exoskeleton** and **Damage Reduction 2**.

##### Sensor Package

(Passive)

A set of advanced sensors is integrated into your helmet. You gain the **Special Senses** power (of your choice) at a Grade equal to your **Exoskeleton Grade**. Using **Special Senses** costs **1 Action** and the **Resolve cost** corresponding to the Grade used.

##### Sealed Suit

(Passive)

The armor is sealed, allowing you to operate in any environment. You can breathe in a vacuum or underwater, and become immune to damage from extreme heat/cold and to the **Burned** and **Frozen** Statuses.

##### Onboard Computer

(Passive)

The suit possesses a tactical A.I. While wearing the armor, you gain the **Tech Knowledge**.

## Technological Spheres

##### 4 PAX/Grade

You are accompanied by a set of high-tech spherical drones that respond to your commands, acting as your eyes, shields, weapons, and loyal companions. The spheres possess **Functions**, And you can have a number of active **Functions** equal to your Grade in that Power. Each one has an Activation and a Cost, as well as a duration, specified in its description.

Identical Functions do not stack. If your Resolve reaches zero, the Spheres are deactivated.

##### Defense Mode

**Activation**: Free

**Cost**: 2 Resolve

The spheres orbit around you at high speed, intercepting attacks. By the end of the Scene, you receive a bonus to your Defense equal to your Grade in this Power.

##### Attack Mode

**Activation:** 1 Action

**Cost**: 2 Resolve

The spheres position themselves for combat, opening their energy emitters. By the end of the Scene, you gain access to the power. Energy Blast with a Grade equal to your Grade in this Power.

##### Propulsion Mode

**Activation**: 1 Action

**Cost**: 2 Resolve

The spheres attach themselves to you, functioning as a propulsion system. You receive the power. Flight (Grade 1) until the end of the Scene.

##### Electromagnetic Pulse

**Activation**: 1 Action

**Cost**: 3 Resolve

The spheres release a pulse that disables nearby technology. Robotic enemies or those with armor that generate technological effects, such as the Exoskeleton, must make a Test of Resistance (Tension 16) or lose access to their technological abilities for 1 Round.

##### Tactical Analysis

**Activation**: 1 Action

**Cost**: 1 Resolve

You control the spheres to scan the area. Until the end of the Scene, you receive Advantage on your next Test of Mind to search for clues, hidden enemies, or technological anomalies in the Zone.
