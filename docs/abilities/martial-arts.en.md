# MARTIAL TRAINING

Martial Training is divided into **3 categories: Martial Master** (unarmed combat), **Swordsman** (melee weapons), and **Marksman** (ranged weapons).

**Techniques**: These powers work differently from the others. At **Grade 1**, the Hero learns **2 techniques** from that Grade’s list. With each new Grade acquired, they may choose **one more technique** from any Grade equal to or lower than their current one. Techniques may be **Active** (requiring Action and/or Resolve) or **Passive** (permanent bonuses). Each technique also has its own Resolve cost indicated in its Grade.

##### Acquiring Techniques

To acquire techniques, a character must have the proper Grade in that Power: at **Grade 1**, they gain access to **two techniques**, and only **one of those two** may have the descriptor **Passive**. After that, each Grade grants **1 new Technique** of a Grade equal to or lower than the one possessed.

## SWORDSMAN

##### 6 PAX / Grade

Whether with a sword, a trident, or a staff, you are a master in the use of melee weapons. To you, a weapon is an extension of your body and your will.

### Grade 1

Activation Cost: 1 Resolve

When acquired, choose **2 techniques** (only **1** may be Passive).

##### Singular Weapon

(Passive)

When fighting with a single type of melee weapon (swords, for example), you add your **Swordsman Grade** to your **Attack Tests** and to your **Defense**.

##### Two-Weapon Fighting

(Passive)

When fighting with a melee weapon in each hand, your Multiple-attack penalty becomes **–4 instead of –5**.

##### Sudden Charge

(1 Action)

You move one distance and attack in the same breath. The attack adds **half your Agility bonus** to the **Attack Test**.

##### Violent Attack

(1 Action)

You sacrifice precision for power. Make a weapon attack, adding **double your Swordsman Grade** as a direct bonus to damage, but with **Disadvantage** on the **Attack Test**.

##### Defensive Stance

(1 Action)

You focus on your defense. Your **Block** action gains an additional bonus equal to your **Swordsman Grade**.

##### Precise Attack

(1 Action)

Make a weapon attack, adding your **Damage Die** as a bonus to your **Attack Test**.

### Grade 2

(Activation Cost: 2 Resolve)

When acquired, choose **1 new technique** of **Grade 2 or lower**.

##### Quick Draw

(Passive)

You gain a bonus to your **Initiative Tests** equal to your **Swordsman Grade**. Your first attack in each Scene gains **Advantage**.

##### Parry Blow

(Reaction)

When you are the target of a melee attack, you may spend your reaction to make an **Opposed Test** and pit your own **Attack** against the enemy’s. If you win the Test, the enemy’s attack is negated.

##### Arc Attack

(1 Action)

You make one attack that hits up to **3 adjacent enemies**, each taking **half your normal Damage**.

##### Tempest of Blows

(2 Actions)

You deliver **three attacks** in rapid succession. The Multiple-attack penalty is –2 for each of those attacks.

## MARTIAL MASTER

##### 6 PAX / Grade

You have turned your body into a living weapon. The Martial Master focuses on the use of bare hands to injure, immobilize, and stun the opponent.

### Grade 1

**Activation Cost: 1 Resolve**

##### Acrobatic Fighter

(Passive)

You add your **Martial Master Grade** to your **Defense**.

##### Martial Artist

(Passive)

You add your **Martial Master Grade** to your **Spirit** and to your **Unarmed Attack Tests**.

##### Brawler

(Passive)

You add your **Martial Master Grade** to your Potency and to the Damage of your **Unarmed Attacks**.

##### Jab-Cross

(Passive)

Your **Multiple Attack** penalty becomes **–4 instead of –5**.

##### High Guard

(1 Action)

You focus on your defense. Your **Block** action gains an additional bonus equal to your **Martial Master Grade**.

##### Precise Strike

(1 Action)

You aim for an opening. Make an **Unarmed Attack**, adding your **Damage Die** as a bonus to your **Attack Test**.

### Grade 2

Activation Cost: 2 Resolve

When acquired, choose **1 new technique** of **Grade 2 or lower**.

##### Body Fortification

(Passive)

You add your **Martial Master Grade** to your **Damage Reduction** and to your total **Resolve**.

##### Fists of Steel

(1 Action)

Your blows become as powerful as steel. Until the end of your turn, your **Unarmed Attacks** deal additional **Damage** equal to your **Martial Master Grade +1**.

##### Acrobatic Kicks

(1 Action)

With a single **Action**, you move one distance and execute a series of kicks against a group of enemies. This attack gains a bonus to the **Attack Test** and to **Damage** equal to the number of enemies surrounding you at the end of the movement. If the enemy has the **Group** descriptor, you gain **Advantage** on the **Attack Test**.

##### Swift Sweep

(1 Action)

You perform a precise sweep that causes no damage against up to **3 adjacent enemies**. The **Attack Test** is made with –2, but all who are hit must make an **Opposed Agility Test** against your **Potency** or gain the “**Prone**” Status.

## MARKSMAN

##### 6 PAX / Grade

Whether with bows, firearms or energy projectiles, you are a master of ranged combat. Every shot is calculated for maximum effect.

### Grade 1

Activation Cost: 1 Resolve

When acquired, choose 2 techniques (only 1 may be Passive).

##### Favorite Weapon

(Passive)

Choose one type of ranged weapon (for example, Bows). When using it, you receive a bonus to your Attack Test and to Damage equal to your Marksman Grade.

##### Special Ammunition

(Free Action)

At the beginning of your turn, you may declare that you are using special ammunition, and you have an amount of this ammunition equal to your Marksman Grade. Choose one of the following effects for your next attack:

**Impact Shot:** If it hits, the enemy is pushed 1 Distance.

**Pinning Shot:** If it hits, the enemy gains the “Slowed” Status for 1 Round.

##### Ricochet Shot

(1 Action)

Make an attack against one target. If it hits, you may cause the projectile to ricochet and strike a second target within a **Close** distance of the first, who suffers **half Damage**.

### Grade 2

Activation Cost: 2 Resolve

When acquired, choose **1 new technique** of **Grade 2 or lower**.

##### Instinctive Shooter

(Passive)

You may make ranged attacks against **Adjacent** targets without suffering **Disadvantage**.

##### Special Ammunition (Improved)

You add new options to your **Special Ammunition** ability:

- **Smoke Shot**: The attack creates a small cloud of smoke at the point of impact, granting cover to the area and causing the “**Blinded**” Status to all who are **Adjacent** to the point of impact until the beginning of your next Turn or until they move away from the point of impact.

- **Boxing Glove Shot**: You are able to fire boxing glove–shaped projectiles. The target must make an **Opposed Resistance Test** against your **Accuracy**. If they fail, the target suffers the “**Stunned**” Status until the end of the target’s next Turn.

##### Barrage of Shots

(2 Actions)

You fire rapidly into an area. Choose one point. You make an attack against up to **3 targets** within a **Close** distance of that point, each suffering **half Damage**.
