# THREATS AND VILLAINS

Not every battle is decided on top of a skyscraper against an evil genius.

Often, justice is forged on dirty asphalt, in abandoned laboratories, and in skies filled with nameless enemies. It is the sound of the claws of an **Arkham Aberration** echoing in an alley, the collective hum of a swarm of **Parademons** descending upon the city, or the empty stare of a **Street Thug** who is only there for the paycheck. These are considered **Threats**, ordinary enemies encountered by Heroes. **Villains**, on the other hand, are dangerous beings worthy of a name.

###### Special Abilities

Some threats and villains possess Special Abilities, which are unique feats that change the course of a battle. While a Villain is able to act at the end of each Hero’s turn, even if they have no Actions left, threats with the Brute descriptor deal more Damage with their attacks.

## VILLAINS

Villains are dangerous and treacherous, and they do not follow the same rules as Heroes: they fight dirty. Whether by taking hostages or stealing candy from children, Villains are the worst! As the Editor, you need to use Villains in a cunning and, to be honest, unfair way.

Villains have **7 Condition boxes.**

#### Captain Boomerang

**Category:** Tier C **Origin:** Human **Villain**

**Protocol:** George “Digger” Harkness operates under the alias **Captain Boomerang**. Using a variety of boomerangs with many different functions, this sarcastic, opportunistic criminal is an excellent marksman. His skill with these unusual weapons gives him an edge: while armed forces may wear bulletproof vests, no one really expects a criminal to use a boomerang in a bank robbery.

Do not underestimate him because of his weapon choice: this villain is extremely lethal and has already been part of several groups beyond the Rogues, such as the **Suicide Squad**.

**Contingency Plan:** Captain Boomerang is, above all, a mercenary. He will never place any objective above his own safety, and he has a tendency to betray his allies in critical situations. ALL of these traits make him predictable in negotiations. Despite lacking superhuman abilities, Harkness compensates with his accuracy and vast arsenal of boomerangs.

His excessive confidence and need to demonstrate superiority can be used against him: a good taunt is capable of completely destabilizing the villain.

**Attributes:** Potency +3, Accuracy +9, Agility +6, Resistance +3, Spirit +6, Mind +3

Resolve: 36

**Defense:** 18 (**Damage Reduction 1 / Damage Reduction 2 against projectiles**)

**Attacks:** Boomerang +15. Damage: 2d6+8 (**Distant / Physical**)

**Powers:** Marksman 3 (Favorite Weapon, Special Ammunition, Ricochet Shot,Improved Special Ammunition )

**Equipment:** Kevlar Suit, Smoke Bomb, Grappling Gun, Boomerangs, Tracker.

###### Special Abilities

**Villain:** Captain Boomerang gains **1 Action** that does not count toward the Round’s action total at the end of each Hero’s Turn.

**Guaranteed Return:** Once per **Round**, when he misses an Attack against a target, at the end of the turn Captain Boomerang may make a new Attack against the same target, dealing **half Damage** on a hit.

#### Talon

**Category:** Tier C **Origin:** Human **Villain Furtive**

**Protocol:** This is the name given to elite assassins of the **Court of Owls**, a secret cult of Gotham City’s elite. Trained since childhood—and sometimes resurrected for that very purpose—a Talon is a silent killer and a skilled fighter. In addition, they are excellent trackers and loyal soldiers, the result of the brainwashing they receive from the Court. They are true urban legends that allow the Court to maintain its control over all of Gotham.

**Contingency Plan:** A Talon is a relentless hunter and will always use the environment to their advantage. Once they have a clear objective, they will go to the end to fulfill it, whatever the cost.

That means concepts such as honor or a fair fight do not exist: when at a clear disadvantage, a Talon will retreat into the shadows. Using false targets to draw the Talon’s attention and only then attacking has already proven effective in some situations.

**Attributes:** Potency +6, Accuracy +9, Agility +9, Resistance +6, Spirit +6, Mind +6

Resolve: 35

**Defense:** 20 (Damage Reduction 3)

**Powers:** Martial Master 3 (Acrobatic Fighter, Precise Strike, Fists of Steel, Body Fortification), Swordsman 2 (Precise Attack, Sudden Charge, Barrage of Blows)

Attacks:

Fists +10. **Damage:** 2d6+6 (Physical) Katana +12. **Damage:** 2d6+8 (Physical)

Throwing Daggers (Close) +8. **Damage:** 1d6+6

**Knowledge and Traits:** Subterfuge, Affiliation (Court of Owls)

###### Special Abilities

**Villain:** Talon gains **+1 Action** that does not count toward the total action count at the end of each Hero’s turn.

**Gift from the Court:** At the beginning of the **Round**, choose one piece of **Gear** from the Equipment list. You may use it once until the end of the Round.

**Shadow Strike:** When hidden in the shadows, Talon gains **+1 die** on their **Damage rolls**.

#### Captain Cold

**Category:** Tier C **Origin:** Human **Villain, Genius**

**Protocol:** Leonard Snart operates as a criminal under the alias of Captain Cold. Possessing a brilliant mind, the villain uses cutting-edge technology, primarily through his powerful Cold Gun, capable of generating extremely powerful freezing blasts to operate in Central City.

While wielding his Cold Gun, he has access to various ice-manipulation abilities creating barriers, freezing blasts, manifesting slippery surfaces, and much more.

**Contingency Plan:** Despite his high destructive power, Captain Cold remains entirely dependent on his Cold Gun.

**Attributes:** Potency +6, Accuracy +9, Agility +6, Resistance +3, Spirit +12, Mind +9

Resolve: 62

**Defense:** 20

**Powers:** Energy Blast G2 (Ice), Elemental Manipulation G2 (Ice), Marksman 3 (Favorite Weapon, Ricochet Shot, Instinctive Shooter, Barrage of Shots)

**Attacks:** Ice Blast +15. Damage 2d6+15 (Close/Ice)

**Knowledge and Trait:** Tech, Pocket Gadgets **Equipment:** Cold Gun: When using the Cold Gun, Captain Cold gains access to Energy Blast and Elemental Manipulation.

###### Special Abilities

**Villain:** Captain Cold gains 1 Action that does not count toward the Round action Limit at the end of each Hero’s turn.

**All Part of the Plan:** Once per Scene, when targeted by an attack, Captain Cold may declare that it was all part of the plan to negate the incoming hit.

**Cold Aura:** Captain Cold gains +2 Defense (already included) and takes half damage from attacks that deal cold damage.

**Ice Age:** Once per Campaign, Captain Cold may completely destroy his Cold Gun, absorbing its powers. When he does so, he gains all of its Powers without needing to wield it, becoming a Meta-Human. Additionally, he gains +4 Defense and 15 Resolve , becoming a Tier B threat.

**Practical Knowledge:** At the beginning of the Scene, Captain Cold may choose one ability from a Knowledge. He receives that Knowledge until the end of the scene.

**Speedster Hunter:** Captain Cold deals +1 Damage Die to characters who possess the Speed Force.

#### Robin

**Category:** Tier: C **Origin:** Human **The Anti-Hero**

The son of Bruce Wayne and Talia al Ghul, Damian Wayne was raised to be the ultimate weapon of the League of Assassins. Now, as the current Robin, he struggles to reconcile his lethal training with his father’s moral code.

Arrogant, violent, and incredibly skilled, Damian is a hero in constant inner conflict, seeking to prove his worth while suppressing his darker instincts.

**Roleplaying Tips**: Damian is what happens when you pair arrogance with lethal precision. He considers himself superior to everyone in the room. Do your best to end battles quickly and brutally.

His journey is about learning to be a hero, not an assassin. Moments of vulnerability, or reluctant respect for his peers, are key to his development.

**Attributes**: Potency +6, Precision +6, Agility +6, Resistance +6, Spirit +3, Mind +6

Resolve: 25

**Defense**: 22

**Attacks**: Katana + 11. Damage 1d6+7 (Physical) Martial Strikes + 7. Damage 1d6+6 (Physical)

**Powers**: Swordsman G2 (Singular Weapon( Ka-tana), Precise Strike,Violent Attack, Parry Blow), Martial Master G2 (Acrobatic Fighter, Precise Strike, Acrobatic Kicks)

*Archetype Skills:*

###### Dirty Boxing

ALL this talk of heroism, playing fair, and being just sounds great on paper, but on the street, the only rule is survival. A dirty trick when your opponent isn’t looking is worth more than a speech full of pretty words. Once per Round, when you hit a target with an Attack, you can spend 2 Resolve Points and an Action to deal +2 Damage per Tier.

###### I Follow Other Rules

You don’t follow the conventional heroic playbook. Once per Issue, when you do something morally questionable, you can use one of the Editor’s Editorial Points as if it were a Plot Point.

**Equipment:** Katana (Martial Weapon), Smoke Bomb (2 Pax)

**Traits:** Nepobaby (Afilliation: Bat-family), Pocket Gadgets (4 Pax)

**Knowledge:** Strategy, Tech, Subterfuge

###### Limitations

**Bad Reputation:** In Rescue scenes, the Resolve cost of abilities increases by 2.

**Paranoia:** Damian suffers from intense paranoia, distrusting everything and everyone around him.
