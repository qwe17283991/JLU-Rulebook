# LIMITATIONS

Every Hero worth the name has a weakness, after all, no one is completely invincible. Acting both as a mechanical weakness and a narrative hook, a **Limitation** is something that makes our Heroes tangible.

Think of the **Martian Manhunter’s** aversion to fire, **Superman’s** vulnerability to Kryptonite or **Batman’s** moral code. When choosing a Limitation, do not think that you are weakening your Hero: it is a chance to make the story more interesting.

##### Choosing Limitations

As a rule, every **Archetype** offers one Limitation, such as the **Enraged’s Side Effect**. But beyond that, every player (with the exception of some Origins) must choose **one more Limitation** for their Hero.

Whenever your Hero is exposed to that Limitation in a narratively important moment, they will suffer a consequence. The **Editor** is the final arbiter of when a Limitation is activated, but players are encouraged to roleplay their own weaknesses and bring them into the story.

##### Consequences of Limitations

Limitations manifest mechanically in several ways. As a rule, Limitations take precedence over other abilities or powers in the game: a Martian with **Vulnerability to fire** does not get rid of that Limitation if they acquire the **Invulnerability** Power to that element. Each type of consequence offers a different challenge:

**Vulnerability:** Applies the **Vulnerable** status when in contact with the target.

*Example:* A Hero of divine origin may have **Vulnerability** to profane or hell-forged weapons.

**Weakness:** You receive a negative **Status** when exposed to a certain condition.

*Example:* A Hero with **Pyrophobia** (fear of fire) may receive the “**Frightened**” Status whenever confronted with a large source of flames, suffering penalties on their actions.

**Nullification:** Your powers are temporarily turned off.

*Example:* A technological Hero may have their **Exoskeleton** power **Nullified** by an **EMP**.

**Debilitation:** You move up one level on your Condition track.

*Example:* A Hero whose physiology depends on darkness may suffer a **Debilitation** when exposed to direct sunlight, immediately marking the “**Tired**” Condition.

**Impossibility:** You are unable to use certain **Knowledge** or **Traits** in specific situations.

*Example: *A character with a rigid **Code of Conduct** against lying suffers **Impossibility** when trying to use any social Test to deceive someone.
