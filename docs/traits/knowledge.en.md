# KNOWLEDGE

A Hero without Knowledge on a particular subject is **UNABLE** to make any Test on that subject. Without the **Piloting Knowledge**, your Hero may be able to drive a car, but will not be able to perform maneuvers or chases, for example, because those would involve **Tests**.

##### Acquiring Knowledge

Each Knowledge costs **4 PAX**, and grants all the abilities listed in that field. Possessing a Knowledge is enough for a Hero to be a considerable force in that area, a true expert.

## Acrobatics

You practically fly through the city. Leaping over buildings, climbing walls, and performing incredibly agile maneuvers are part of your everyday life.

**Climbing**: When climbing any surface, leaping over buildings, or moving acrobatically, you gain Advantage on your Test.

**Acrobatic Jump**: Once per **Issue**, you gain an **automatic success** when performing an unbelievable feat of jumping or aerial movement.

**Free Running**: When you need to cross an area with obstacles (rooftops, walls, gaps), reduce the **Tension by 5** to reach your destination quickly.

## Subterfuge

Going unnoticed, opening locks, and avoiding direct confrontation. **Subterfuge** is the art of not being seen and always having an ace up your sleeve.

**Slip By**: When making **Agility Tests** to sneak around, you gain **Advantage**.

**Escapist**: No bond can hold you. Once per **Issue**, if you are imprisoned or handcuffed, you may escape automatically.

**Unaware Strike**: Once per Scene, when dealing damage to a target that is unaware of your presence, you roll **1 additional Tier Damage Die**.

## Tech

You are an expert in electronics, programming and advanced devices. You know how to hack, repair, and create last-minute gadgets.

**Hacker:** If you have the appropriate tools, you may break into sophisticated systems. You gain **Advantage** on Tests involving hacking systems and breaking codes.

**Tech Rat**: When you spend a **Scene** observing and studying a technological item, you completely understand how it works.

**Technological Gadget**: You carry a special gadget. At the beginning of the **Issue**, choose one **Gear** item from the list to receive. It must be used by the end of the **Issue**.

## Strategy

You are a master of tactics and planning. You can foresee movements, elaborate maneuvers and wild plans like no one else.

**Plan B**: You always have a backup plan. Once per **Issue**, when you fail a Test, you may describe how you had a plan up your sleeve and reroll the Test, keeping the second result.

**Maneuver Number 7!**: You prepared for this moment. At the beginning of a **Scene**, choose one Attribute on a single target character. They receive a bonus equal to your **Tier** on their next roll with that Attribute.

**Tactical Analysis**: Once per **Issue**, you may quickly analyze your target. You must choose one of the following factors about the target to discover: a **Weakness**, their **Resolve Points**, one **Attribute** of your choice, or the first action they will take on their turn.
