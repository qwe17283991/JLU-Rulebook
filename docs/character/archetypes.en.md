# ARCHETYPES

Each Archetype grants access to unique traits and special abilities that reflect its philosophy. Think of it as the ‘why’ behind your superheroics, the motivation that drives your Hero to act. Choose carefully, because your Archetype will guide the way you interact with the world and the way the world reacts to you.

When you choose your Archetype, at **Tier D** you gain **two** abilities from it.

## Champion of Tomorrow

You carry hope wherever you go. As the **Champion of Tomorrow**, your presence inspires civilians, motivates allies, and makes villains hesitate. The community expects great things from you and the eyes of the world are always upon you. Be the beacon in the darkness and a symbol of justice.

**Examples**: Superman, Black Lightning and Wonder Woman.

##### Code of Conduct

The Champion of Tomorrow’s Code of Conduct is what guides your heroism. This code is your compass and if you break it, you lose all of your **Resolve** points. Choose one of the options below:

**Set the Example**: You do not use excessive force in the fight against villainy. Whenever you leave a villain **Wounded**, you must offer them a chance to surrender.

**Render Unto Caesar**: You follow the law of humankind and may not place yourself above justice. You must always obey the law and follow lawful orders.

**Protection**: You must always prioritize the rescue of innocents. As long as people are in danger in the game, you must save them before doing anything else.

##### Champion of Tomorrow Archetype Abilities

Inspiring Presence

Once per **Round**, if your allies can see or hear you, you may spend **3 Resolve** to inspire them. ALL allies gain a bonus equal to your **Tier** on their next roll.

No One Gets left Behind!

Once per Scene, when an ally or civilian would suffer **Damage**, you may instantly move to them, intercepting the blow or hazard that would harm them and taking the Damage in their place. Regardless of the amount of Damage, you mark one **Condition**.

Classic Heroism

In a **Rescue Scene**, you do not spend **Resolve** when taking your first action. This ability ignores the rule that every Activation must cost at least 1 Resolve.

Truth and Justice

You have a nose for lies and corruption. Once per Scene, you may spend **4 Resolve** to count as having scored an automatic success on Tests involving revealing the truth, exposing the unseen, or uncovering corruption.

## Dark Knight

You have learned to use shadows and darkness to your advantage. As a shadowy vigilante, you fight in the night using stealth tactics, martial arts and specialized gear. Perhaps you are a member of the Bat-Family, or perhaps you come from a lineage of shadow assassins. Either way, you are fast, precise and deadly.

**Examples**: Nightwing, Batman and Midnighter.

##### Feet on the Ground

While others fly, shoot beams from their eyes, and command the elements, you have other ways of fighting villainy. The only Powers a **Dark Knight** may have above **Grade 1** are **Martial Training** and **Technology**.

##### Dark Knight Archetype Abilities

I Am the Night

Once per Scene, as long as no one is looking at you and you are not restrained or prevented from moving, you may disappear and reappear in another adjacent Zone.

Martial Artist

You gain Grade 1 in Martial Training.

Utility Belt

You have a belt with exactly the right gear for any situation. Need to reach the top of a building quickly? You have a grappling gun. Need to lose your enemies? Smoke bomb them. Once per Scene, you may spend **3 Resolve** to gain a suitable item that helps you overcome a challenge narratively (night-vision goggles, a grappling line, a gas mask and so on).

Prep Time

You possess special training, a contingency plan, or some other extremely useful skill that helps you at the last possible moment. Once per **Issue**, when you use your **Prep Time**, you gain **1 Plot Point**.

## Genius

In a world of powerful aliens and capricious deities, you know that the mind is the most powerful weapon of all. Your brilliant intellect has taken you to places that even the most fantastic powers could not. You know that planning is essential to action and having an ace up your sleeve never hurts.

**Examples**: Mr. Terrific, The Atom, and Lex Luthor.

##### Overthinking

Sometimes you freeze while considering the countless possible outcomes. You suffer **Disadvantage** on your **Initiative** rolls.

##### Genius Archetype Abilities

Checkmate

Everything is clear, like a chessboard. Once per Scene, you may spend 1 Action to move an ally who is within the same Zone as you. You may move them out of danger, place them strategically between yourself and an opponent, or simply put them in the perfect position for an attack.

It’s Trivial!

Whenever you come across an artifact, device, vehicle or something similar, you automatically understand the basics of how it works. Given enough time, you can also repair any kind of mechanism created on Earth.

Technological Knowledge

You gain 1 Grade in a Technology Power.

Blabblalogy

Once per **Issue**, when you encounter advanced technology, an ancient tale, a strange substance or something similar, you may launch into an extended scientific explanation. When you do, you gain **1 Plot Point**.

## Enraged

Raw, concentrated power, charging at the enemy like a true wrecking ball on the battlefield. The **Enraged** is a living weapon of war, ready to go all out and unleash all of their fury.

**Examples**: Big Barda, Kilowog, and Hawkman.

##### Collateral Damage

You hit first and ask questions later. In a Scene, you cannot help civilians until the threat has been completely neutralized.

##### Enraged Archetype Abilities

Everything in the Ring

Choose one enemy who is fighting you. Until the end of the Scene, whenever the two of you fight each other, both of you gain **Advantage** on **Attack** and **Damage** rolls.

Chain Breaker

You can tear through structures as though they were nothing. Once per Scene, spend **2 Resolve** to break a barrier, such as a steel gate, mystical bindings or reinforced walls.

Red with Rage

Once per Scene, when you mark Wounded, you gain a bonus equal to your **Tier +2** on your **Attack** and **Damage** rolls until the end of the **Scene** or until you recover from that Condition.

Cardboard World

Once per **Issue**, when you face a situation in which you can unleash the full extent of your powers—such as a powerful enemy or an almost insurmountable obstacle, deliver a speech about how you no longer need to hold back. Your **Damage Die** increases by one step until the end of the **Scene**.
