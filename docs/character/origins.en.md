# ORIGIN

A Hero’s **Origin** represents their starting point. It is a combination of biology, culture, and upbringing. Perhaps they came from a distant planet that was destroyed, were created in a laboratory or descended from a noble lineage beneath the ocean.

**Power Kit**: Each Origin offers Heroes a unique list of predefined powers, called a **Power Kit**. During Hero creation, the player may choose **one Grade** in a Power from that Kit for free.

**Buying Other Powers**: Can a Kryptonian master electric powers? Can an Amazon be touched by the Speed Force? Yes. Although uncommon, it is not impossible for a Hero to gain powers that are not typical of their Origin. Whenever this happens, the power costs **double the PAX per Grade**. **Humans** are the exception to this rule and cannot buy any powers from outside of their Power Kit.

## Amazon

Fueling countless legends, the Amazons are a civilization of female warriors originating from the island of Themyscira, with roots in ancient Greece. Raised by the gods of the Olympian pantheon, Amazons are trained from a young age in the arts of war and strategy, such as archery, swordsmanship, and hand-to-hand combat.

##### Amazon Origin Powers Kit

- Super Strength

- Enhanced Resistance

- Superhuman Speed

- Combat Training

##### Amazon Equipment

You receive an Amazon Armor that grants you DR equals your Tier+1, and an Amazon Sword that grants you +2 on your Attack rolls and +1 in Damage.

##### Divine longevity

Amazons are beings created by deities, and can live forever if they are not harmed. Every Amazon receives the Perennial Trait.

##### Sisters in Arms

You receive the Affiliation (Themyscira) Trait.

##### Perennial (Trait)

Time has no power over you. Whether by divine blessing, a scientific accident, or your unique biology, your body has stopped aging. You are immune to its effects. It is a natural process and one cannot die of old age.

**I remember that!**: Once per Issue, when you come across a historical figure, event, or culture, you can state that you remember it. The Editor will tell you an important fact about that figure.

## Human

##### Human Power Kit

- Martial Training

- Technology

##### Only Human

Humans cannot purchase Powers outside their Power Kit.

##### Versatility

Your Hero gains one Knowledge and one Simple Trait.

## Kryptonian

##### Kryptonian Origin Power Kit

- Energy Blast

- Invulnerability

- Enhanced Resistance

- Enhanced Senses

- Super Strength

- Super-Breath

- Superhuman Speed

- Flight

##### Blood of Krypton

You gain a bonus to **Potency** and **Damage Reduction** equal to your Tier. Whenever your Tier increases, both the bonus and the Damage Reduction increase as well.

##### Limitations

When exposed to green Kryptonite, Kryptonians lose access to all their powers and become Vulnerable. Unless they are under the effects of a yellow sun, Kryptonians lose their Powers.

## Meta-Human

Meta-Humans are individuals who possess the **meta-gene**, a mutation capable of granting its bearer a vast range of abilities. Since no two meta-genes are alike, the variety of powers available to Meta-Humans is virtually limitless. They come in all types, shapes, and sizes, and are most commonly found on planet Earth.

##### Meta-Human Power Kit

Meta-Humans do not receive a free Power. Instead, they may purchase any Superpower at its normal cost.
